Current Season 2025 Season Definitions

Metric Definitions

Weekly Score

The total points scored by a manager's starting lineup in their head-to-head matchup for a given week.

Cumulative Average Score

A running average of a manager's weekly scores across all completed matchups. This shows how scoring output trends over the course of a season.

Luck %

A measure of how a manager's actual matchup result compares to their expected results against the rest of the league. It is calculated by taking the actual win % (100% for a win and 0% for a loss) and subtracting the win/loss percentage against other teams.

  • Example 1: If a manager had the 2nd highest weekly score but played against the highest, their luck % for that week would be -100%; and conversely, a manager with the 2nd lowest weekly score who played against the lowest would have a luck % of 100%.
  • Example 2: The managers with the highest and lowest scores each week have 0% luck, as their actual results did not rely on their matchups.
  • Example 3: A manager who scored higher than 5 others but lost their matchup would have -55.56% luck, as they would have received a win against 5 out of 9 teams.

Cumulative Wins

A manager's total number of head-to-head matchup victories as the season progresses.

Luckiest Manager

The manager with the highest luck % in a given week, which highlights a manager who won their matchup despite a comparatively low score.

Unluckiest Manager

The manager with the lowest luck % in a given week, which highlights a manager who lost their matchup despite a comparatively high score.

Overperformer

The manager who exceeded their own cumulative average score by the largest margin in a given week, which highlights the biggest individual boom week relative to a manager's established baseline.

  • Note: This award goes to the highest scoring manager in week 1, as all cumulative average scores are 0.

Underperformer

The manager who fell short of their own cumulative average score by the largest margin in a given week, which highlights the biggest individual bust week relative to a manager's established baseline.

  • Note: This award goes to the lowest scoring manager in week 1, as all cumulative average scores are 0.
