Current Season 2025 Season Definitions

Metric Definitions

Weekly Score

The total points scored by a manager's starting lineup in their head-to-head matchup for a given week.

Cumulative Average Score

A running average of a manager's weekly scores across all completed matchups. This shows how scoring output trends over the course of a season.

Luck %

A measure of how a manager's weekly matchup result (win/loss) would change if they had competed against the rest of the league. It calculates how many other teams a manager would have beaten in a given week and assigns a percentage for how (un)lucky that manager's matchup was.

Example: If a manager had the 2nd highest weekly score but played against the highest, their luck % for that week would be -100%; and conversely, a manager with the 2nd lowest weekly score who played against the lowest would have a luck % of 100%.

Cumulative Wins

Your total number of head-to-head victories as the season progresses.

Luckiest Manager

The manager with the highest positive difference between their actual head-to-head result and their expected wins against the rest of the league. This often highlights a manager who won their matchup despite a low weekly score.

Unluckiest Manager

The manager with the lowest negative difference between their actual result and their league-wide performance. This typically highlights a manager who scored very well but still lost because they played the highest-scoring team of the week.

Overperformer

The manager who exceeded their own cumulative season average by the largest margin in a given week. This highlights the biggest individual boom week relative to a manager's established baseline.

Underperformer

The manager who fell short of their own cumulative season average by the largest margin. This highlights the biggest individual bust week relative to a manager's established baseline.
